ANY-PRESENCE isn't code-signed, so Windows or your antivirus may warn about it. The code is public in the GitHub repository, so you can read it, have someone you trust review it, or build your own copy.

Privacy

What stays on your computer

Your settings, My Games list, saved apps, hours played and small copies of banners are saved in %APPDATA%\ANY-PRESENCE. If you add an ImgBB key, it is saved there too, as plain text. Nothing is sent to the author, and there are no accounts, ads or analytics.

To find the game you're playing, the app reads the names and icons of the programs running on your computer. That list stays on your computer. Only the program you choose is used, and only the title, status, buttons and images you set are shown on Discord.

Start with Windows

If you turn it on in Settings, the app adds itself to your Windows startup list. Turning it off removes it.

The website demo

The demo on the website is a copy of the app that runs in your browser. Your searches go to the App Store and VNDB directly, and to Steam and GOG through a small proxy that doesn't store anything. Nothing is connected to Discord, and nothing you do is saved. The demo keeps a daily request counter in your browser, and the website remembers your chosen theme in your browser, and both can be cleared in your browser settings.
