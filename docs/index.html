What it does

Everything you can change, you can change.

Search the storesSteam, GOG, the App Store, VNDB, DLsite, Wikipedia, Lutris and the Internet Archive fill in the title and cover.
Any programNo store has your game? Pick the running program. Its icon is added automatically.
Your own iconsDrop, paste or pick any image, then zoom, move and rotate it. The status line is yours to write.
ButtonsAdd up to two buttons to your presence, like "Get on Steam". Other people see them.
My GamesLaunch a game in one click, keep your hours played, and give every game its own banner.
Saved appsSave a program once. The presence starts when it opens and stops when it closes.
Game infoDescriptions, trailers, screenshots and ratings from the store, right inside the app.
ThemesFive to choose from, plus one you design with your own three colors.
Stays out of the wayHide it in the system tray, and let it start with Windows if you like.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it need Discord to be open?

Yes, the Discord desktop app has to be open on the same computer, with Activity Status on. If it's closed when you press Start, the game is still added to My Games, and the presence starts as soon as Discord opens.

Is it free? Do I need an account or a key?

It's free, and there's nothing to sign up for. ANY-PRESENCE comes with its own Discord application. A free ImgBB key is optional: it only lets your own images and program icons show on Discord.

Windows says it's a virus. Is it?

No, but Windows and some antivirus programs often warn about new apps like this one. The app isn't code-signed (that costs money), it can start with Windows, and it reads your running programs to find the game you're playing. All of that makes antivirus programs wary.

You don't have to take my word for it: all of the code is public, so you can read it, have someone you trust review it, or build your own copy. If Windows blocks it, click More info, then Run anyway.

What does it send over the internet?

Everything runs on your computer. The app only contacts the game stores you search, ImgBB (only if you add a key), Discord, and GitHub (to check for updates). Your settings, My Games list and hours played stay on your PC.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not for now. ANY-PRESENCE is for Windows only.

Ready to show what you're playing?

One file, no installer. Download it, open it, press Start.
